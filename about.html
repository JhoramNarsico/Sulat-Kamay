From Our Hands to Yours

A simple guide to capturing your memories.

01
🎁

Select & Personalize

Visit the Shop. Choose a curated box (Love, Cute, or Flowers) and add optional items like chocolates or polaroids.

02
✍️

Write Your Message

Use our Letter Builder to type your message. We will handwrite this exact text on vintage paper, sealed with wax.

03
🧾

Get Your Slip

Go to Cart > Checkout. Enter your nickname. Our system will generate an Order Slip (and Letter Slip) image. Save/Download these images.

04
📨

Submit Order

Send the saved images to our Instagram @sulatkamay_ph. We will reply with payment details to confirm.

📸 How to send the Order via Instagram

Once you have downloaded your Order Slip (and Letter Content) from the Cart:

  1. Open Instagram and search for @sulatkamay_ph.
  2. Tap the Message button to open our chat.
  3. Tap the Image Icon (🖼️) at the bottom left of the chat.
  4. Select the Order Slip image you just downloaded.
  5. (If you wrote a letter) Select the Letter Content image as well.
  6. Hit Send! We will review it and send you the payment details (GCash/GoTyme/Bank).
